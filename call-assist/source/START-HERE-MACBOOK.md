# Start here

On your iPhone, open https://247agenttech.com/call-assist/, enter your name, then tap Start and allow microphone access after confirming consent. Try sample tests real AI without a microphone. Expand conversation details to correct speakers and export notes.

The backend is hosted: once published, your Mac can sleep or shut down while the iPhone app continues to work. Keep the iPhone page in the foreground while listening. It hears nearby speech, including a separate speakerphone; it cannot directly capture iPhone telephone-call audio.

For development, use `npm start` with Node.js 22.9+. Keep `.env` private. See README.md, ROADMAP.md and docs/DEPLOYMENT.md for the directory, current scope and deployment details.
